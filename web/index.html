Everything you need to know about display safety, power assertions, and installation.

Dopamine uses macOS IOKit power assertion APIs (such as PreventUserIdleSystemSleep) to keep the kernel and background processes running, while registering a low-level DisplayServices hardware notification. When clamshell closure is detected, Dopamine immediately signals the display controller to cut power to the backlight LEDs, achieving genuine 0.0 nits true black without putting the SoC to sleep.

Liquid Retina XDR screens contain thousands of high-intensity mini-LEDs. When legacy tools keep the Mac awake with the lid closed, those mini-LEDs remain continuously energized at high nits inside a sealed, unventilated air gap right against your keyboard keys. Over time, trapped heat can damage delicate anti-reflective coatings and LCD layers. Dopamine physically cuts backlight power to 0 nits, keeping the screen cool.
